How to buy a ballpen

  1. Wake up the machine The screen shows Good day! Would you like a pen? then Press PURPLE. Press the purple button to start.
  2. Choose how many pens The screen shows How many pens? Press green to add one and red to remove one (1 to 10). Press purple to confirm.
  3. Check the price The machine shows the total for a moment, for example 2 pcs = P20
  4. Insert the exact amount The screen shows Insert exact P30 and the amount inserted so far. Drop your coins in the coin slot one at a time.
  5. Collect your ballpen When the amount is complete, the screen shows Dispensing... and the pens drop into the chute. Take them from the pickup slot at the bottom.
  6. Done The machine says thank you for buying and goes back to the start screen.
Please note: the machine cannot give change. If you insert more than the total, the extra money is kept, so please insert the exact amount.

The three buttons

Green: ADD
Adds one pen to your order.
Red: MINUS
Removes one pen. At the payment screen it cancels your order, only if no coin has been inserted yet.
Purple: CONFIRM
Starts your purchase and confirms your choice.

Questions and answers

My coin came back out. What do I do?

The coin slot checks each coin. A dirty, bent or foreign coin may be rejected. Wipe it and try again, or use another coin.

I put in coins but not enough. What happens?

The amount stays on the screen. Keep inserting coins until it matches the total. If you leave, the money stays as credit for the next purchase.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel with the red button at the payment screen, but only before any coin has been inserted. After that, please complete the purchase.

Why can I only choose up to 10 pens?

That is the limit for one purchase. If fewer pens are left in the machine, you can only choose up to what is left.

The screen is not responding.

If you do nothing for 3 minutes, the machine goes back to the start screen. If it still does not respond, please tell the owner.

Tip: if you close the door without pressing purple, the change is discarded and the old stock count stays. Always press purple to save.

Having a problem?

If the machine does not work, a coin is stuck, or something looks wrong, please send us a message and tell us what happened.
